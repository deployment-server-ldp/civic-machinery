<p>If one event defines the tobacco industry calendar each year, this is it. Intertabac and InterSupply 2026 run from September 15 to 17 at Westfalenhallen Dortmund in Germany, and this edition brings a real change worth planning around: for the first time, the show is moving off its usual Thursday-through-Saturday run to a Tuesday-through-Thursday schedule. Now in its 46th year, the fair is expected to draw more than 800 exhibitors and around 14,500 trade visitors from roughly 70 to 80 countries.</p><h2>The Changing Face of the Tobacco Industry</h2><p>The tobacco industry has shifted more in the past decade than in several before it. Alternative products, from e-cigarettes to heated tobacco devices, have changed what a show like this actually needs to cover. Intertabac 2026 reflects that directly, with its exhibition halls split into distinct sections for cigarettes, cigars, pipes, smoking accessories, and next-generation products. For anyone attending, that split gives a fairly direct read on where consumer demand is moving and what that means for sourcing and production decisions heading into 2027.</p><h2>Trends and Insights Worth Tracking</h2><p>Staying current on where the industry is heading isn't optional for manufacturers anymore. Alongside the exhibition floor, the show runs technical sessions and market discussions covering regulatory shifts and where reduced-risk products are trending next. For machinery buyers in particular, this is often where the most useful information actually surfaces, lead times, new equipment launches, and what's genuinely moving through the supply chain, details that tend to come across more clearly in a floor conversation than in a press release.</p><h2>What to Expect at Intertabac and InterSupply 2026</h2><p>Three days, one venue, and most of the relevant names in the industry under the same roof. Here's what that looks like once you're actually there.</p><h3>Key Features and Exhibitors</h3><p>Intertabac 2026 brings together a wide spread of exhibitors, giving visitors the chance to compare suppliers side by side instead of relying purely on outreach from a distance. InterSupply, held in the same halls, is where the manufacturing side of the industry shows up specifically, covering production equipment for tobacco products, e-cigarettes, pipes, and shisha for a dedicated B2B audience.</p><h2>The Role of Technology at Intertabac 2026</h2><p>Technology keeps reshaping this industry, from how machinery is engineered to how packaging is designed, and a show of this scale is where a lot of that gets shown publicly for the first time each year.</p><h3>Innovations on Display</h3><p>Expect live equipment demonstrations rather than static booths, alongside software built to streamline production operations. Watching a machine actually run tells you something a spec sheet on its own never quite can.</p><h3>Networking Opportunities</h3><p>With close to 14,500 trade visitors across three days, and roughly half of them arriving from outside Germany, this is one of the more genuinely international touchpoints the tobacco industry sees all year.</p><h2>How Industry Professionals Benefit</h2><p>Face-to-face conversation still drives a large share of how business actually happens in this industry, and a show of this size compresses months of separate outreach into three working days.
