The HLP-180 does this with a single plunger. The HLP-200 and HLP-225 use a double plunger system, which pushes the cigarettes into the pack shell from two coordinated points rather than one.</p><p>This matters at higher speeds. A single-plunger design can struggle to keep pack density and alignment consistent once output climbs past a certain point. The double plunger design is part of what lets the HLP-200 and HLP-225 hold their rated speed without a rise in rejected or poorly formed packs.</p><p>At 180 packs per minute, the single-plunger HLP-180 still runs steady, efficient packing cycles with consistent alignment, wrapping and sealing on every pack, which makes it a dependable choice for high-volume hard pack production for local and export markets.</p><h3>Match the Packer to Your Cigarette Maker</h3><p>The most common mistake on a cigarette line is a packer slower than the maker.
