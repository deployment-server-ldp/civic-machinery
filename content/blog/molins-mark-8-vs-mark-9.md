<p>The Molins Mark 8 family runs at 1,800 to 3,000 cigarettes per minute and is lighter and simpler. The Mark 9 family runs at 3,500 to 5,000 and comes with larger filter assemblers and tray fillers. For a first line or a single shift, the Mark 8 usually makes more sense. Once you need well over a million cigarettes a shift, the Mark 9 pays for its extra weight and power.</p><p>Both families are widely used, parts are available, and reconditioned units are common. The Mark 8 Post 64 uses the Max Multiroll, a proven attachment for steady mid-range output. The Mark 8D steps up to a MAX-15 with an 1117 infeed unit. The Mark 9 machines pair with a Max-S filter assembler and a tray filler, which loads finished cigarettes into trays automatically and takes manual handling out of the line. At 5,000 cigarettes a minute, that automation is not optional.</p><h3><strong>Floor, Power and People</strong></h3><p>A Mark 9 line weighs more than twice a Mark 8 Post 64.
